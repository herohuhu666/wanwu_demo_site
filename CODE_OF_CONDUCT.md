# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to keep discussion respectful, specific, and focused on improving the project.

Examples of welcome behavior:

- clear bug reports and reproduction steps
- constructive code review
- respectful disagreement
- documentation improvements

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- harassment or personal attacks
- spam
- publishing private information
- intentionally disruptive behavior

## Enforcement

The maintainer may close issues, remove comments, or block users who repeatedly violate these standards.
